# KEYWORDS.md — naming variants and search terms

A **finding aid**, not a set of claims. Listing a term here does not assert that
it names a real person, a real event, or a genuine document — several entries
below are precisely the things this project records as *disputed*. Read
`context.md` for what is contested.

Per `sourcing-rules` ("Absence is a claim"): before reporting that a corpus or a
source says nothing about something, search **every** variant listed here, pair
the zero with a positive control from the same corpus by the same method, and
write down which corpus and which method were used. Accents and the ç in
*Huei tlamahuiçoltica* break naive searches; so does the hard-wrapped line.

Note the trap that has its own disambiguation entry in the dataset: **Guadalupe
(Tepeyac, Mexico) ≠ Guadalupe (Cáceres, Extremadura, Spain)**. Sixteenth-century
Spanish references to "Guadalupe" often mean the Extremaduran shrine. Never
count a bare "Guadalupe" hit as Mexican without reading the context.

## Nahuatl terms and names (keep as they are — never translate)

- **Tepeyac** — the hill; also *Tepeyacac*, *Tepeaquilla*, *Tepeaca* (colonial
  Spanish spellings; *Tepeaca* is also an unrelated town in Puebla — check).
- **Cuauhtlatoatzin** — Juan Diego's Nahuatl name; also *Quauhtlatoatzin*,
  *Cuauhtlatoazin*, *Guauhtlatoatzin*. Search with and without the initial *C/Q*.
- **Nican Mopohua** — "here it is told"; the apparition narrative. Also
  *Nikan Mopoua*, *Nican mopohua* (lowercase second word is common).
- **Huei tlamahuiçoltica** — the 1649 Nahuatl tract containing it. Variants:
  *Huei tlamahuizoltica*, *Hvei tlamahuiçoltica*, *Huey tlamahuizoltica*.
  The **ç** is the single most common cause of a false zero here — always run a
  *z* variant too.
- **Nican Motecpana** — the miracle accounts printed alongside the Nican Mopohua.
- **Tonantzin** — "our revered mother", the Nahua title of a goddess venerated at
  Tepeyac before the conquest, per Bernardino de Sahagún. The claim that the
  Guadalupe cult continued or replaced a *Tonantzin* cult is **contested**: it is
  argued by some historians of religion and rejected by defenders of the
  tradition. The term is listed here so the literature can be searched, not
  because this project endorses the identification.
- **Cuautitlán / Cuauhtitlan** — Juan Diego's reported home town; the 1666
  testimonies were taken there. Also *Quauhtitlan*.
- **náhuatl** (ES) · **náuatle / náhuatl** (PT) · **Nahuatl** (EN) — the language.

## Terms known to be traps

- **"Guadalupe"** alone — two shrines, two continents (see above).
- **"aparición"** in colonial Spanish can mean the *appearance* of the image as
  an object, not a vision. Read the sentence.
- **"1531"** appears in later documents as a *retrospective* date; a hit is not
  evidence of a 1531 source.
- **Julian vs Gregorian**: the 1531 dates are Julian. Sources almost never say
  which calendar they use. Do not silently convert.
